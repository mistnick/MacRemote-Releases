MacRemote 0.1.2 includes the remote input fix and improves startup when Screen Recording is denied on a Mac used as a viewer.

- The WebRTC Metal video view no longer intercepts remote pointer, click, and keyboard events.
- A local Screen Recording denial no longer blocks startup as a viewer; screen capture remains required on the Mac being shared.
- Apple Silicon, macOS 14 or later.
- Developer ID signed and accepted by Apple notarization; ticket stapled and validated.

Open the DMG and drag MacRemote into Applications. To share a Mac, grant Screen Recording and Accessibility to MacRemote in System Settings > Privacy & Security on that Mac.
