MacRemote 0.1.0 is an early access installer for remote Mac testing.

- Native macOS app for Apple Silicon, macOS 14 or later.
- Signed with Developer ID and accepted by Apple notarization.
- The DMG includes the app and an Applications shortcut.

Install by opening the DMG and dragging MacRemote into Applications. At first launch, grant Screen Recording and Accessibility permissions as needed.
